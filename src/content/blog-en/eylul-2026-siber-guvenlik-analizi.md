## The key message

2026 is the first year where AI in cybercrime stopped being just a tool and became part of the attack architecture itself. Agents coordinate with each other, generate and mutate code, and outpace human operators. Defenders have not yet closed that gap.

This post examines the most critical cybersecurity events of September 2026: compromise of an FBI wiretap system, attacks by swarms of AI agents, leakage of 284 million patient records, Qilin as the most active ransomware operation, a tragedy in the Berlin Senate, and more.

Incidents in public institutions highlight that cybersecurity failures have real-world consequences beyond IT.

## Defensive priorities

- Treat AI agents as privileged automation: constrain credentials, network egress, and tool permissions.
- Assume supply-chain and SaaS exposure; inventory and rotate secrets.
- Invest in detection engineering for anomalous automated behavior, not just malware signatures.
